How it works

  1. 01

    Pick your patch

    Type a town or share your location. Coordinates are rounded to about 1 km before they leave your computer.

  2. 02

    Read the neighbourhood

    We pull recent iNaturalist sightings and the 7-day forecast, and flag what has just showed up. Your local model writes the tips.

  3. 03

    Print it. Go.

    One page, ink-friendly, with tick boxes. Leave the phone at home and tick things off as you spot them.

Open on purpose

  • Private by default

    The model runs on your laptop. Nothing about you is stored on a server.

  • Free forever

    Open data and an open-weight model: no API key, no bill, no tier to outgrow.

  • Swap the model

    One environment variable changes the model or the server (Ollama, llama.cpp, LM Studio, vLLM).

  • Works on the trail

    Once it is printed, the trail needs no signal at all.
