How it works

Drop. Fix. Post.

  1. 01

    Drop it

    Any video or voice note. It is decoded on your own machine and never sent anywhere.

  2. 02

    Fix it

    Whisper listens, we write it in Roman Hinglish. Click any line to fix a word and the timing follows.

  3. 03

    Post it

    Pick a style, burn the captions in, or grab an .srt. Ready for Reels, Shorts and WhatsApp status.

Why open models

Built for a side hustle, not a subscription.

Hinglish that looks right

Devanagari scares off half your audience. We convert it word by word, keep English words as English, and let you teach it your own spellings.

Your files stay put

Transcription runs on your own CPU or GPU. Client footage, voice notes and drafts never touch a server you do not control.

Models you can swap

Tiny for quick drafts, large-v3-turbo for the final cut, or your own Hindi fine-tune. No per-minute pricing, ever.
